Privacy policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Covenant Couples is built for privacy. This page describes what the app does with information in plain language. It is not legal advice. Questions: michael@walkingriver.com.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, email, or name to use the app.
  • We do not run in-app advertising or sell personal data.
  • We do not read your shared messages—Share uses your device’s own share sheet; we never receive a copy.

On this device

Your session (context, preferences, and the suggestion you see) is held in memory while you use a flow. It is cleared when you finish a session the app way (e.g. Done on the suggestion screen) or you start a fresh path from the home screen. We do not upload a “profile” of you to our servers.

A small setting is saved in your browser (local storage): whether you allow optional AI suggestions. It has no name or contact data—just that on/off choice, so it persists between visits.

If you use AI suggestions, we also store a random anonymous id locally (a UUID) so repeat requests from your device can be routed efficiently by our AI provider. It is not your name, email, or an advertising identifier.

Optional AI

If you leave Allow AI suggestions on (in About) and the app is built with a proxy URL, the app may send a single structured JSON request to our Cloudflare Worker containing the same kind of data you already entered in the app (preferences, context, and matcher metadata). The worker calls an AI provider to return text. Turn the toggle off to use only built-in templates—then no request is sent for AI.

That network path is subject to the privacy practices of Cloudflare (hosting the worker and often the static app) and the AI provider used by the worker, in addition to your own network and device settings.

Share & copy

When you tap Share, the visible suggestion text (including any invite line and link we add) is passed to your operating system or browser share UI. Who receives it is entirely your choice. When you Copy, text goes to your clipboard locally.

PWA & technical notes

As a Progressive Web App, the browser may cache files so the app loads offline or faster. That is normal browser behavior. Hosting may log technical data (such as IP address for HTTPS requests) like any website.

Children

The app is intended for adults in a marriage context. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If our practices change in a meaningful way, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
