Privacy policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

UsTonight is built for privacy. This page describes what the native iPhone and Android apps do with information in plain language. It is not legal advice. Questions: michael@walkingriver.com.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, email, or name to use the app.
  • We do not run in-app advertising or sell personal data.
  • We do not upload your preference profiles to our servers for matching.
  • We do not read your shared messages—Share uses your device’s own share sheet; we never receive a copy.

On this device

Your session (context, preferences, and the suggestion you see) is held in memory while you use a flow. It is cleared when you finish a session the app way (e.g. Done on the suggestion screen) or you start a fresh path from the home screen. We do not upload a “profile” of you to our servers.

A small setting is saved on your phone: whether you allow optional AI suggestions. It has no name or contact data—just that on/off choice, so it persists between visits.

Optional on-device AI

If you turn on Allow AI suggestions in About, the app may use your phone’s on-device AI (where supported by your device and operating system) to refine a suggestion. Processing stays on your phone—nothing is sent to us for AI enhancement.

On devices without on-device AI support, the built-in template matcher still works. Turn the toggle off to use only built-in templates.

Share & copy

When you tap Share, the visible suggestion text (including any invite line and link we add) is passed to your operating system share UI. Who receives it is entirely your choice. When you Copy, text goes to your clipboard locally.

This website

This marketing site may log standard technical data (such as IP address for HTTPS requests) like any website. It does not receive your in-app session data.

Children

The app is intended for adults in a marriage context. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If our practices change in a meaningful way, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
